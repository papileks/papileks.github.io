SUWE

the Nigerian hopscotch game

Toss your pebble, hop the court,
and never touch a line!

Best score: 0

How to play

  1. Toss — tap (or press Space) to stop the power bar inside the marked band and land your pebble in the glowing target box. Land on a line or in the wrong box and you foul!
  2. Hop out — tap when the slider is inside the green zone to hop from box to box. You must jump over the box holding your pebble.
  3. Hop back — at the top, turn around and hop home. On the way back you stoop to pick your pebble up from the box beside it.
  4. Wobbles — one bad tap is a wobble. Two wobbles in the same run means you stepped on a line: foul!
  5. Finish boxes 1 to 6 to win. In 2-player mode a foul passes the turn — first to clear box 6 takes the game.

Suwe is a beloved Nigerian playground game, traditionally scratched into sand or drawn in chalk. Players take turns tossing a pebble into numbered squares and hopping through on one foot — touch a line and your turn is over.

You win!
