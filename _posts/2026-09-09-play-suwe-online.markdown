Suwe — the classic Nigerian playground game — is now playable right here on this site.

Toss your pebble into the numbered box, hop through the court on one foot, jump over the box holding your pebble, turn at the top, pick it up on the way back, and hop out. Touch a line and you foul, just like on the playground.

Play solo for a high score, or grab a friend for pass-and-play: a foul hands over the turn, and the first to clear box 6 wins.
