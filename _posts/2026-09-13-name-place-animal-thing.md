The classic paper-and-pencil word race, now playable in the browser:
**[Play Name, Place, Animal, Thing](/npat/)**.

Pass one device around: everyone gets the same letter, races to fill in a
Name, a Place, an Animal and a Thing, and the fastest finisher takes the
Stop crown. Unique answers score 10, duplicates 5, and the review screen is
where the arguments happen — tap any answer to rule it in or out.

House rules are configurable: custom categories, rounds, timer, letter
pool, point values, stop bonus, and how strictly duplicates are matched
(exact, normalized, or fuzzy typo-catching).
